Applications

Where a metal skin goes

The scenes use the texture and metal you have selected. Vertical joints mark the width of one roll in the selected texture's size.

Illustrative scenes. Texture scale and colour are indicative and should be confirmed against a physical sample.

Feature walls

Full-height cladding in 1.75 ft widths, for living rooms, lobbies and corridors.

Shutters and doors

Wardrobe, cabinet and entrance door faces skinned edge to edge.

Columns and curves

The skin bends, so round columns and curved counters take metal without fabrication.

Headboards and panels

Framed panels behind a bed, a console or a reception.

Furniture

Table aprons, drawer fronts, niches and shelving backs.
